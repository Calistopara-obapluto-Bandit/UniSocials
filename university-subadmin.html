🏦 Campus pool — 55% of the platform's fee (45/55 split)

Here is the money chain, in full: every verified sale at your universities pays the platform's fee first — the event's authorised rate (20% by default; 25/15/13/10 when a different plan is set; referred sales contribute their 2.5%). That fee is then split 45 / 55 — the platform keeps 45%, and 55% comes back to your campuses as this pool. Example: a ₦5,000 sale takes a ₦1,000 fee → the platform keeps ₦450, your campuses get ₦550 (11% of the sale). You handle payment requests from your campuses (💼 Workers); your own pool draws go to the Main Admin, who reviews them and oversees everything.
