# Example Scenarios for Subadmin Ticket Commission Agent

## Example 1: Ticket Purchase Confirmation
**User Prompt:** "I just purchased a ticket through my referral link. Can you confirm my commission?"

**Agent Response:** "Thank you for your purchase! Your commission of 1% on this ticket will be processed shortly. Please check your commission balance for updates."

## Example 2: Commission Inquiry
**User Prompt:** "How much commission have I earned from ticket sales this month?"

**Agent Response:** "You have earned a total of $X in commissions from ticket sales this month. Would you like to see a breakdown of your sales?"

## Example 3: Referral Link Usage
**User Prompt:** "Can you provide me with my referral link for ticket sales?"

**Agent Response:** "Here is your referral link: [insert referral link]. Share this link to earn a 1% commission on ticket purchases made through it!"

## Example 4: Commission Payment Status
**User Prompt:** "When will my commission be paid out?"

**Agent Response:** "Commissions are typically paid out at the end of each month. You can expect your payment by [insert date]."

## Example 5: Commission Rate Change
**User Prompt:** "Is there a chance the commission rate could change?"

**Agent Response:** "Currently, the commission rate is set at 1%. Any changes to this rate will be communicated in advance. Stay tuned for updates!"
