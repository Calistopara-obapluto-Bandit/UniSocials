# Commission Workflow for Subadmin Ticket Purchases

## Overview
This document outlines the workflow for calculating and issuing a 1% commission to subadmins for ticket purchases made through their referral links. It serves as a guide for understanding how commissions are tracked, calculated, and distributed.

## Workflow Steps

1. **Referral Link Generation**
   - Subadmins generate unique referral links for ticket purchases.
   - Each link is associated with the subadmin's account to track sales accurately.

2. **Ticket Purchase Tracking**
   - When a customer clicks on a referral link and completes a ticket purchase, the system logs the transaction.
   - The transaction details include the subadmin's ID, ticket price, and timestamp.

3. **Commission Calculation**
   - Upon successful purchase, the system calculates the commission:
     - Commission = Ticket Price * 0.01
   - This calculation is performed in real-time to ensure accuracy.

4. **Commission Issuance**
   - The calculated commission is credited to the subadmin's account.
   - A notification is sent to the subadmin confirming the commission earned from the sale.

5. **Reporting**
   - Subadmins can view their commission earnings through a dedicated dashboard.
   - Reports are generated periodically to summarize total commissions earned over a specified period.

## Example Scenario
- A subadmin shares their referral link on social media.
- A customer clicks the link and purchases a ticket for $100.
- The system calculates a 1% commission of $1, which is credited to the subadmin's account.

## Conclusion
This workflow ensures that subadmins are fairly compensated for their efforts in promoting ticket sales. By automating the commission calculation and issuance process, we enhance transparency and encourage subadmins to engage actively in sales activities.
