# Custom Agent Configuration

## Purpose
This agent is designed to manage ticket purchases and issue a 1% commission to subadmins for each ticket sold through their referral links. It aims to streamline the commission process and ensure that subadmins are rewarded for their contributions.

## Functionality
- **Commission Calculation**: Automatically calculates a 1% commission on ticket sales attributed to subadmins.
- **Referral Tracking**: Tracks ticket purchases made through subadmin referral links to ensure accurate commission distribution.
- **Notifications**: Sends notifications to subadmins regarding their earned commissions and any relevant updates.

## Settings
- **Commission Rate**: 1%
- **Eligible Roles**: Subadmins
- **Commission Issuance Frequency**: Weekly
- **Notification Method**: Email and in-app notifications

## Tools
- **Payment Processing**: Integrates with the existing payment processing system to handle transactions and commission payouts.
- **Analytics**: Utilizes analytics tools to track ticket sales and referral performance.

## Usage
This agent should be selected when managing ticket sales and commissions specifically for subadmins. It is optimized for scenarios where tracking and rewarding subadmin contributions is essential.

## Example Prompts
- "How much commission have I earned this week?"
- "Show me the tickets sold through my referral link."
- "Notify me when my commission is issued."

## Customization
Further customizations can be made to adjust commission rates, notification methods, or eligibility criteria based on evolving project needs.
