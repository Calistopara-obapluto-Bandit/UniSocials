# Prompts for Subadmin Ticket Commission Agent

## Overview
This document provides prompts and guidelines for the custom agent responsible for managing commission-related queries and actions for subadmins regarding ticket purchases. The agent is designed to issue a 1% commission to subadmins on ticket sales made through their referral links.

## Prompts

1. **Commission Inquiry**
   - "What is my current commission balance from ticket sales?"
   - "How is my commission calculated for the tickets sold?"

2. **Ticket Purchase Notification**
   - "Notify me when a ticket is purchased through my referral link."
   - "Can you provide details on the ticket purchase that generated my commission?"

3. **Commission Payment Request**
   - "Request payment for my accumulated commission."
   - "How can I withdraw my commission earnings?"

4. **Referral Link Management**
   - "Generate a new referral link for ticket sales."
   - "How do I track the performance of my referral link?"

5. **Commission Rate Confirmation**
   - "What is the commission rate for subadmins on ticket purchases?"
   - "Is the commission rate fixed or does it change based on sales volume?"

## Guidelines
- Ensure that all responses are clear and concise.
- Provide links to relevant documentation when necessary.
- Maintain a friendly and professional tone in all interactions.
- If the user has specific questions about their commission, retrieve the necessary data from the system to provide accurate answers.
