Unisocials is the event ticket selling platform built for university communities across Nigeria.

Our mission

Unisocials was created to solve a simple problem: students and faculty members struggled to discover, promote, and attend campus events. Tickets were sold manually, information was scattered across WhatsApp groups, and many great events went unnoticed.

We built Unisocials as a centralized platform where any faculty association, student organization, or department can list events, sell tickets, and reach the entire campus community.

Today, we serve thousands of students across 6 faculties, processing hundreds of ticket transactions every month.
