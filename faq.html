Frequently asked
questions

Everything you need to know about buying tickets, listing events, and using Unisocials.

How do I buy a ticket?
+
Simply browse the events page, select the event you're interested in, and click "Buy Now" on the tickets page. You'll be guided through the checkout process. After payment, your digital ticket will be sent to your email and phone.
What payment methods are accepted?
+
We accept all major payment methods securely through Flutterwave â€” including debit/credit cards, bank transfer, USSD, and mobile money. Your payment is processed by Flutterwave, our trusted payment gateway.
Can I get a refund if I can't attend?
+
Refund policies vary by event. Some events offer full refunds up to 48 hours before the event, while others are non-refundable. Please check the event-specific terms before purchasing. Contact support for assistance with refund requests.
How do I list my event on Unisocials?
+
Simply fill out the contact form or email us at support.sbiamautos@gmail.com with your event details, and our team will set everything up for you.
Is there a fee for selling tickets?
+
There is no fee to list events on Unisocials. We charge a small service fee (5%) on each ticket sold to cover payment processing and platform maintenance. This fee is included in the ticket price shown to buyers.
How will I receive my ticket after purchase?
+
Your digital ticket will be sent via email and SMS immediately after payment confirmation. You can also access your tickets anytime by visiting the "My Tickets" page on our platform. Simply show the QR code at the event entrance.
Can I transfer my ticket to someone else?
+
Yes, tickets are transferable. You can transfer your ticket to another student by contacting support at least 24 hours before the event. The new attendee will receive a fresh QR code for entry.
What if I lose my ticket QR code?
+
Don't worry! You can request a resend from the "My Tickets" page, or contact support and we'll resend your ticket to your email and phone immediately. We recommend saving a screenshot of your QR code after purchase.

Still have questions?
We're here to help.

Reach out to our support team and we'll get back to you within hours.
